# OptiFine Alternatives
I do not officially support OptiFine.
It breaks like every mod and since it's closed-source, it is extremely frustrating and time-consuming to fix bugs caused by it.

Instead you should use Oculus/Iris and Rubidium/Sodium.
It's so much more stable and doesn't break everything.

**ON FORGE, USE:**
- Oculus (Shaders): https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/oculus
- Rubidium (Performance): https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/rubidium

**ON FABRIC, USE:**
- Iris (Shaders): https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/irisshaders
- Sodium (Performance): https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/sodium
