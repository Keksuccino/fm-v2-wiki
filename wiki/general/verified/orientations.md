# 1. How To Use Orientations

Orientations are the **anchor point** of your elements.
Elements will always calculate their positions by using their orientation as starting point.

So if your element has the `top-left` orientation and is 2 centimetres away from the **top left** corner of the window, it will **always** be 2 centimetres away from said corner, no matter what size your window has.

Choose the best fitting orientation for your element.

> **NOTE**: If your layout looks bad after **resizing the window** or **changing the menu scale**, you're probably using the wrong orientations for your elements.
{.is-info}

You can change the orientation of an element by **right-clicking** it and clicking on **Orientation** in the [Layout Editor](./layout-editor).
