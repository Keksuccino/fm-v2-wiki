# How to Become a Wiki Author

You work a lot with FancyMenu and have good knowledge about it?
You like writing and explaining stuff?

Well, that sounds great, because this wiki is now partly community-driven and everyone can contribute to it!

All you need to do is **sending me a PM in Discord (Keksuccino#0425)** with a short text about your history with FancyMenu and why you want to become a wiki author.

Thank you so much for your interest in contributing content to the wiki!
You are great and what you're doing will help a lot of people, including me! <3

# Different Types of Authors

<br>

## Junior Author

Every author starts as a **Junior Author**.
This means they can **add and edit pages** in `/wiki/general/unverified` and `wiki/dev/unverified`.
These are the directories of all pages written by normal authors.
Because every author can edit pages in there, it is possible that it contains misinformation and that's why these pages are tagged as "unverified", because it is impossible for me to check all of them.

## Author

Once a **Junior Author** wrote some good pages, they eventually become an **Author**, which means they are now able to also **remove, rename and move pages** in the `unverified` directories. That's an upgrade!

## Senior Author

Well and then there is the type of author that just can't stop doing great work.
These are quite special, because they can become **Senior Authors**, which means they have ***ultimate power***! In other words, they have full access to both the `unverified` **AND** `verified` directories and can add, remove, edit, rename and move pages in there.
